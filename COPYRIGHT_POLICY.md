# Copyright and Full-Text Policy

Public availability is not a license. Do not commit Campbell, Miller, HyperGuide, subscription articles, publisher figures, copied question banks, or other protected full text merely because it can be downloaded or viewed.

The default public artifact is bibliographic metadata plus a stable landing-page URL. Full text or figures may be committed only when an explicit license permits redistribution, the exact license and source URL are recorded, attribution is preserved, and the asset contains no restricted or identifiable clinical information.

When rights are uncertain, store only metadata, DOI, and retrieval instructions. Automated collection must respect source terms, robots and rate limits where applicable, and access controls. Never bypass authentication or technical restrictions.
