# Medical Research Workflow

1. Register a stable project ID and formulate a PICO or PECO question.
2. Search registries and recent reviews to assess novelty without treating a quick search as proof of novelty.
3. Freeze a dated Protocol and Statistical Analysis Plan before outcome-driven analysis.
4. Build and peer-review database-specific search strategies.
5. Export raw records without alteration; log query, filters, hit count, filename, date, and checksum.
6. Normalize metadata into a derived layer and deduplicate conservatively.
7. Perform auditable title/abstract and full-text Screening with explicit reasons.
8. Extract data with source locations and independent verification for decisive fields.
9. Apply design-appropriate Risk of Bias tools and reporting guidance.
10. Run the prespecified analysis, record deviations, and keep reproducible outputs.
11. Draft the Manuscript from verified results; never invent citations or outcome values.
12. Verify current official Author Guidelines immediately before submission.

Automation may organize evidence, but eligibility, clinical interpretation, Risk of Bias, and final scientific claims require accountable review.
