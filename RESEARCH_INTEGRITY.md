# Research Integrity

- Never fabricate studies, citations, participants, methods, outcomes, effect sizes, P values, confidence intervals, approvals, registrations, or author instructions.
- Separate copied source text, structured extraction, reviewer judgment, and model-generated suggestion.
- Record source locations and provenance for decisive extracted values.
- Preserve conflicting evidence and protocol deviations rather than rewriting history.
- Use current design-appropriate reporting and Risk of Bias frameworks; mark applicability decisions explicitly.
- Obtain ethics, consent, registration, and data-governance approvals when the project requires them. Repository automation is not ethical approval.
- Treat automated screening, extraction, and clinical interpretation as reviewable assistance, not accountable authorship or final scientific judgment.
- Correct material errors visibly and retain an audit trail.
