## Trial registration gate

For trials that meet ICMJE's definition, plan registration in an acceptable public registry at or before first participant consent/enrolment. Source: https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/publishing-and-editorial-issues/clinical-trial-registration.html (accessed 2026-08-12).

## Bias-assessment router for evidence synthesis

- Randomized trials: RoB 2 current design-specific version, https://www.riskofbias.info/welcome/rob-2-0-tool/current-version-of-rob-2
- Non-randomized intervention studies: ROBINS-I; record the exact version because Version 2 was still described as being refined, https://www.riskofbias.info/welcome/robins-i-v2
- Diagnostic studies: use an appropriate diagnostic risk-of-bias tool in addition to STARD; reporting quality is not risk of bias.

Do not reproduce proprietary or restrictively licensed checklists in repository templates. Link to the official source and record the version used.
