# ACL Remnant-Preservation Pilot

This dated pilot exercises only capabilities that are implemented and tested: database-specific query generation and conservative metadata deduplication.

## Question

Among patients undergoing primary ACL reconstruction, does tibial remnant preservation improve postoperative proprioception compared with standard remnant resection?

## Reproducible inputs

- `question.json` contains the PICO terms and explicitly keeps comparator and outcome terms out of the sensitive primary search.
- `records.json` contains public bibliographic or registry metadata only. One DOI-equivalent duplicate is intentional so the deduplication path is exercised.
- `search_strategy.json` is the exact offline CLI output for the committed configuration.
- `search_log.csv` states that none of the four candidate database queries was executed; it contains no invented hit count or export.
- `provenance.json` binds the dated pilot inputs and outputs to SHA-256 checksums and records their access basis.
- `decision.json` records a dated human novelty gate; it is not generated by a score.

## Pilot decision

The broad systematic-review question is a No-Go in its current form because directly overlapping reviews were published in 2022, 2024, and 2025. The 2025 review searched through November 2024 and included proprioception. A 2026 retrospective cohort is a reason to monitor the field, but not sufficient by itself to duplicate the broad review.

The defensible next step is to reframe the question and repeat the novelty check before protocol registration. A candidate direction is whether objectively graded remnant quality or continuity modifies proprioceptive recovery; that candidate remains unverified and is not presented as a publishable claim.

## Boundaries

This folder contains no full-text article, patient data, extracted outcome values, Risk of Bias judgment, or pooled result. The frozen metadata supports an engineering and workflow pilot only.
